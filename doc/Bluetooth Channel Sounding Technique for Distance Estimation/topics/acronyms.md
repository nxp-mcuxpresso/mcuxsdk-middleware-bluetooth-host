# Acronyms

The following acronyms are used in this document.

|Acronym|Description|
|-------|-----------|
|CS|Channel Sounding|
|PDE|Phase-based Distance Estimation|
|RTP|Round-Trip Phase|
|RTT|Rount-Trip Time|
|ToF|Time of Flight|
|MD|Measuring Device|
|RD|Reflector Device|
|EDLC|Early Detect Late Commit|
|SFD|Start-of-Frame Delimiter|
|T_FCS|The RF circuitry locks to the measurement channel (f_i). Devices can also use this time for additional internal calibrations, if necessary. The initiator may start to ramp up its output signal toward the end of this step.|
|T_FM|Time for Frequency Measurement|
|T_SY|Time for Synchronization sequence (CS packet)|
|T_IP1|Interlude Period 1 - transition from TX to RX and the other way around when transmitting in mode 1 (Tn-Tn)|
|T_IP2|Interlude Period 2 - transition from TX to RX and the other way around when transmitting in mode 2 (Tn-Tn)|
|T_GD|Transition time between packet and tone|
|T_RD|Ramp-Down time for transmission|
|T_PM|Single-antenna Phase Measurement period|
|N_AP|Number of Antenna Paths|
|T_SW|Antenna-Switching period|
|AGC|Automatic Gain Control|
|ADC|Analog-to-Digital Conversion|
|CDE|Complex Distance Estimation|
|IFFT|Inverse Fast Fourier Transform|
