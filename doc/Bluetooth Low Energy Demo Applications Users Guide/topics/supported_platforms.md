# Supported platforms

The following platforms support the Hybrid \(dual-mode\) Bluetooth Low Energy and Generic FSK application:

-   KW45B41Z-EVK
-   KW45B41Z-LOC
-   KW47-EVK
-   KW47-LOC
-   FRDM-KW43
