**Connection Handover (Monitoring Active)**

When Anchor or Packet Monitoring is already active on the Target device, the Connection Handover bypasses Time Synchronization and Anchor Search. The Source device suspends TX, retrieves and sends the Handover Data, and the Target device performs the handover connect on the next monitor event. See the [Connection Handover](ConnectionHandoverProcedure.md) section for the detailed step-by-step procedure and signaling chart.
